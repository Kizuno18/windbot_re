<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Auto-Switch Vocation Profiles</span> will automatically switch for the best Targeting Profile if you change character.</p><p>The new profile will be chosen based on new character's Level and Vocation.</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Cavebot </span>is the part responsible for handling the 'auto-walking'.</p><p>WindBot allows you to set several <span style=" font-style:italic;">Waypoint Sections</span>, this way you can keep your waypoints in the most organized way as possible.</p><p>For example, you can set a section for walking on the 'First Floor' of a cave, a section for walking on the 'Second Floor', another one for 'Go to City', and so on...</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Condition Healer</span> is used to heal your character under certain conditions, like paralyze, or poison. It's the second highest priority.</p><p>It can also be used to configure auto-hasters, or auto-manashielders.</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Diagonal Movement </span>is used to allow the Targeting System to move diagonally in the map. This is option can increase the CPU usage a little bit, only use it if really necessary.</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Healer</span> is the part of the bot responsible for healing your character. It's configured based on <span style=" font-style:italic;">healing rules.</span> You can either use instant spells, runes or potions.</p><p>There are three kinds of spell healing:<br/><span style=" font-style:italic;">Spell Healer:</span> used to heal your character when your life goes low. It has the highest priority of the three healers.<br/><span style=" font-style:italic;">Condition Healer: </span>used to heal your character under certain conditions, like paralyze, or poison. It has the second highest priority.<br/><span style=" font-style:italic;">Mana Trainer:</span> used to spend mana when your mana goes too high, this way you won't waste mana on mana training. There are several ways to spend your mana points, check the <span style=" font-style:italic;">mana trainer</span> section for further details. It's the lowest priority of the three healers.</p><p>On most of the servers you are able to use potions at the same time as using instant spells. So, the <span style=" font-style:italic;">Potion Healer</span> runs in parallel with the instant spell healers.</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Looting</span> helps you making loot lists, and choose which items to pick from each corpse.</p><p>WindBot allows you to set several <span style=" font-style:italic;">Looting Profiles. </span>It might be useful for making scripts adaptable for different vocations.</p><p>For example, Knights have a lot of carrying capacity, so they can carry items that doesn't worth much. While Sorcerers and Druids should only pick items that are worth more.</p><p>Based on the WindBot's database, you can make your loot list in just a few steps: <br/>1. Make your <span style=" font-style:italic;">Targeting List </span>(check the Targeting Section for further details)<br/>2. Go to the desired <span style=" font-style:italic;">Looting Profile</span>.<br/>3. Click on <span style=" font-style:italic;">Auto-Generate Loot List.<br/></span>4. Choose the items you want to loot, and you are ready!</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Potion Healer </span>is used to heal your character with health potions, mana potions or antidote potions.</p><p>On most of the servers you are able to use potions at the same time as using instant spells. Therefore, the <span style=" font-style:italic;">Potion Healer</span> runs in parallel with the instant spell healers.</p><p>Be careful, stronger potions must have higher priority (stay on top of the list).</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Spell Healer</span> is used to heal your character when your life goes low. It has the highest priority of the three spell healers.</p><p>Be careful, stronger healing spells must have higher priority (stay on top of the list).</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-style:italic;">Targeting </span>is the part responsible for automatically fighting creatures. You can set a different rule for each creature, and the bot will find the best movements to kill the monsters, according to your rules.</p><p>WindBot allows you to have different <span style=" font-style:italic;">Targeting Profiles</span>, so you can adapt your scripts for each vocation and level.</p><p>For example, Knights needs to fight close to their targets. While Paladins should run from them.</p></body></html>
<html><head/><body><p><span style=" font-weight:600;">Smart Looting:</span> Will open bodies around you whenever your character is standing by for a few seconds. Further bodies will get looted after you kill all the monsters.</p><p><span style=" font-weight:600;">Loot After All Dead:</span> Will wait until you kill all the monsters on screen before going after corpses.</p><p><span style=" font-weight:600;">Loot After Melee Kill: </span>Will open any bodies around your character, even if you are targeting a monster. Further bodies will get looted after you kill all the monsters.</p></body></html>
